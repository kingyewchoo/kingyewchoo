# King Yew Choo

King Yew Choo’s work on payments, AI agents and financial regulation has been published by the House of Lords, Federal Reserve and Financial Stability Board. For the UN’s International Telecommunication Union, he has authored payments use cases to support its focus group on trust and identity in agentic AI. He serves on the British Standards Institution’s financial services committee. Before founding True Primary, he traded emerging-market credit at Citigroup. True Primary builds the infrastructure through which AI agents commission and pay for primary research.

- [Payment and permission checks for agent-initiated work](https://kingyewchoo.co.uk/research/agent-payment-gating/): individual IETF Internet-Draft, revision 01, 8 October 2026
- [A payment and entitlement gate for AI agents purchasing expert services](https://kingyewchoo.co.uk/research/payment-and-entitlement-gate/): SSRN working paper, version 2.9, September 2026, not peer reviewed
- [A transaction architecture for AI agents purchasing expert services](https://kingyewchoo.co.uk/research/agentic-expert-access-architecture/): earlier SSRN working paper, June 2026, not peer reviewed
- [Sandbox tests for AI agents that make payments](https://kingyewchoo.co.uk/responses/house-of-lords-sandbox-testing/): on 12 October 2026, he submitted written evidence to the House of Lords Financial Services Regulation Committee, which published it as RSB0009
- [Thirteen responses to public consultations in 2026](https://kingyewchoo.co.uk/writing/), including submissions to HM Treasury, the Bank of England and the Federal Reserve
