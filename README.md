# King Yew Choo

King Yew Choo’s work on payments, AI agents and financial regulation has been published by the House of Lords, Federal Reserve and Financial Stability Board. He has authored payments use cases for the United Nations’ ITU focus group on trust and identity for agentic AI. He serves on the British Standards Institution’s financial services committee. Before founding True Primary, which builds payment infrastructure for agentic commerce, he traded emerging-market credit at Citigroup.

King Yew Choo leads True Primary and is among the early leaders on agentic payments, how AI agents pay. He writes as an authority on agentic payments: an individual Internet-Draft at the IETF, a working paper on SSRN, and responses to the Financial Stability Board, FDIC, Federal Reserve and FTC. He brings a heavyweight capital-markets record, years trading emerging-market credit at Citigroup, to AI and fintech. A senior voice in AI and fintech, he takes part in the Model Context Protocol’s Financial Services Interest Group.
